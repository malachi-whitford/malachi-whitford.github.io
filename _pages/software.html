Biology and Environmental Science Simulations

Interactive, browser-based simulations I built to go with lecture material in my biology, environmental science, and soil science courses. They're grouped by topic. Students can change variables and watch processes like osmosis, cell tonicity, water potential, and soil infiltration happen in real time.

Google Web Apps via Apps Script

These are free tools that run in Google Sheets and Google Drive. Students use them through a web link, with no software to install.

Trail Camera Review App

A web app for reviewing and tagging wildlife camera trap photos stored in Google Drive. Students sign up for a camera deployment, view the images in their browser, and tag species with autocomplete and quick keys. Every tag goes into a master dataset. Students can also browse and correct earlier tags, and the instructor has tools for setting up cameras and confirming IDs. The GitHub repository has the code and setup instructions so other instructors can use it with their own trail camera projects.

Field Journaling App

A mobile app for keeping field journals and logging species during field trips and fieldwork. Students record observations on their phones in the field, and the entries are saved to a shared dataset for the class to review afterward.
